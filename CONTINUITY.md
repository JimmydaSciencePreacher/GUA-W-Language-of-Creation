# Research Continuity

## Repository identity

**GUA-W Language of Creation** is a new research repository. Its scientific investigation sequence begins at **001**. It is not a continuation of an earlier numbered investigation series.

## Purpose

Investigate and describe scientific observations using a shared foundational vocabulary first recognized through Genesis 1–2, while preserving scientific terminology, evidence, methods, mechanisms, uncertainty, and competing explanations.

## Continuity principles

1. Start with the scientific phenomenon and its established terminology.
2. Preserve observations, measurements, methods, mechanisms, and uncertainty.
3. Apply GUA-W vocabulary only where it clarifies a real relationship.
4. Use the Four-Question Study Method recursively to examine the inquiry, the GUA-W source relationship, the subject, and the Witness.
5. Distinguish shared mechanisms from comparable relationships and analogies.
6. Preserve unresolved questions and counterexamples.
7. Do not import findings or numbering from another repository as established results in this new sequence.
8. Keep all repository writing within this repository's authorized scope.
