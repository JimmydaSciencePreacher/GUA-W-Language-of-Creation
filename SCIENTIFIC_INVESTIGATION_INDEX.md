## Next proposed investigation

**013 — Magnetic Fields in the Interstellar Medium: Field Geometry, Polarization, Turbulence, Tension, and the Movement of Matter.**

This proposed investigation will examine how magnetic fields participate in the structure and dynamics of interstellar gas, and how observations distinguish field orientation, field strength, magnetic stress, and magnetized turbulence.

## Indexing standard

Each entry identifies:
- investigation number and title;
- primary scientific discipline(s);
- central phenomenon or question;
- status (in progress, reviewed, or archived);
- link to the full investigation.

An investigation may connect to other fields, but cross-disciplinary links must state whether they identify the same mechanism, a comparable relationship, an analogy, or an unresolved resemblance.
